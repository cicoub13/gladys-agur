# Changelog

All notable changes to the Agur integration for Gladys Assistant.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- **Requires Gladys 5.3 or later.** The integration is now listed in the catalog
  categories of French Gladys instances only (Agur is a French water
  utility); it stays findable through the catalog search everywhere.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-10-13

### Changed

- Maintenance release: dependency and build updates, no functional change.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-11

### Added

- First release: connect your Agur customer account, and follow each water
  contract in Gladys: meter index, consumption since the previous reading,
  average daily consumption, invoice amount and amount due.
- Creating a device fills its charts with the whole history of readings and
  invoices kept by the customer website.
- Requires Gladys 4.86.0 or later.
